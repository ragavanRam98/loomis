# Story: product-detail

As a shopper, I want to see a product's images, price, offers and buy actions, so that I can decide and purchase.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Product image(s) with thumbnails, title, rating, price with discount, and an offers list.
- Clear "ADD TO CART" and "BUY NOW" actions.
- A specifications section.
- A back control that returns to Home.
