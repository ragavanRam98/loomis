# Story: home-landing

As a shopper, I want a familiar marketplace homepage, so that I can search, browse categories, and jump straight into deals.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A blue header with logo, a prominent search bar, Login, and Cart.
- A category strip and a hero banner/carousel.
- At least two "deal" sections, each a horizontal row of product cards with price and discount.
- Clicking a product card opens its Product detail page.
