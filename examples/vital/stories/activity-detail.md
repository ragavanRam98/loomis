# Story: activity-detail

As an active user, I want to see my steps and calories over time with charts, so that I can understand my trends.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Activity shows a weekly bar chart of daily steps with the week total.
- A Day / Week / Month toggle switches the range.
- A calories-burned chart and key metrics (distance, floors, calories) are shown.
- The back chevron returns to the Dashboard.
